# Competitor reference: O2F Info (o2finfosolutions.com)

Reviewed 5 Nov 2026. A structural summary only, kept for context. **Do not copy text from this site.** All Sarthak copy in `content/` was written fresh. Client logos and testimonials from the competitor were deliberately left out.

## Their site structure
Home, About, Services (Cloud Services, Data & Analytics, App Development, IT Consulting, Telecom Engineering), Careers, Contact. Contact lists four country offices with a three-field form. Careers lists two developer roles and asks for CVs by email. The footer copyright reads 2019, so the content is dated.

## What we took (as ideas, rewritten)
- **Cloud (Salesforce):** strategy, Sales/Service Cloud setup, custom development in Discovery → Prototype → Development → Launch phases, data migration, integration with ERP/finance/marketing/call-centre tools, ongoing support, flexible models from one resource to turnkey, onshore/offshore blend. → `content/services/cloud-services.md`
- **Data & Analytics:** legacy modernisation, advanced/predictive analytics with use cases, big-data DevOps (Spark, Hadoop, Scala/Python/Java), platform support, advisory and PoC/PoV, a Define/Implement/Realise/Grow model. → `data-analytics.md`
- **App Development:** theirs is telecom OSS/BSS specific; we generalised it to custom web, mobile and enterprise apps and kept "order, billing and service-management systems" as experience. → `application-development.md`
- **IT Consulting:** people/process/data/technology framing, a four-step method, a team of specialists (PMP, change, BPA, security, access, cloud strategy). → `it-consulting.md`
- **About:** values (innovation, customer centricity, process, timely delivery), vision and mission, and "how we measure ourselves" beyond revenue. → `content/pages/about.md`
- **Careers:** informal culture, ownership, shared success, benefits (pay, leave, insurance). → `content/pages/careers.md`

## What we dropped
- Telecom Engineering (client decision)
- "Our clients" logo wall and testimonials
- Their hero slider (auto-advancing sliders are on our forbidden list)

## Where Sarthak goes further than them
Recruitment & staffing (IT, non-IT, permanent, contract, sourcing & screening) and HR & payroll services, none of which the competitor site covers. These lead the Sarthak site.
