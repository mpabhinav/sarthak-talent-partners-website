This policy explains how Sarthak Talent Partners Private Limited ("Sarthak", "we", "us") collects and uses personal information through this website.

## What we collect

- **Enquiries:** your name, work email, phone number, company and the details you share about your hiring, technology or payroll needs.
- **Job applications:** your name, contact details, city, experience, notice period, CV link or file, LinkedIn profile, and any salary details you choose to share.
- **Usage data:** anonymous, aggregated information about how the site is used (pages visited, device type), collected through Vercel Analytics, which does not use cookies.

## How we use it

- To reply to your enquiry and provide the services you ask about
- To consider you for roles, contact you about suitable openings, and share your profile with a prospective employer **only with your agreement**
- To improve this website
- To meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## Who we share it with

- Service providers who help us run the site and receive form submissions ([[Web3Forms]], [[Vercel]]), under appropriate safeguards
- Prospective employers, for candidates, only after you've agreed
- Authorities, where the law requires it

## How long we keep it

Enquiries: up to [[24 months]] after our last contact. Candidate profiles: up to [[24 months]], or until you ask us to delete them.

## Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by emailing hello@sarthakco.in. We'll respond within [[30 days]].
